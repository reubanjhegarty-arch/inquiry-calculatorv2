Questions people ask

Is Inquiry Calculator free to use?

Yes. Every calculator and the AI solver are free, with no account, sign-up or download required.

Can it solve a maths problem from a photo or a screenshot?

Yes. Use the Snapshot button on the AI page to capture a browser tab, or attach a photo of a worksheet, and the AI will read the problem and explain the working step by step.

Does the scientific calculator work in degrees or radians?

Both. The Deg/Rad key switches the trigonometry mode, and results update as you type.

Are the loan and savings calculators accurate?

They use the standard amortisation and compound interest formulas used by lenders, so they are a close estimate. Fees, offset accounts and rate changes are not included.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to phones and tablets, and the keypad is sized for touch.

One calculator, every job

Most people keep three tabs open to do one sum: a basic calculator for quick arithmetic, a scientific calculator for trigonometry or logarithms, and a spreadsheet for a loan or a savings goal. Inquiry Calculator puts them on one page. You start on the basic keypad, scroll once, and the same calculator slides over and expands into a full scientific keypad with sin, cos, tan, log, ln, square roots, powers, factorials and π — so the number on your screen carries straight across.

Need money answers instead of numbers? The finance tab has a loan and mortgage repayment calculator, a savings calculator with monthly contributions and compound interest, and a tip split calculator for the end of a meal. If you are stuck on the method rather than the arithmetic, the AI solver will work through the problem with you, and the snapshot tool can read a question straight off a browser tab.
