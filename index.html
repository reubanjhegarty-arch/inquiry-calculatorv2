Percentages

How to work out a percentage change

Subtract the old number from the new one, divide by the old number, then multiply by 100. Going from $120 to $150 is a 25% rise; going back down to $120 is a 20% fall, because the starting point changed.

change % = (new − old) ÷ old × 100

Health

What your BMI actually means

BMI compares weight with height using a single number. It is a rough screen, not a diagnosis — muscle, age and body shape all shift it. Under 18.5 is underweight, 18.5–24.9 is the healthy band, 25–29.9 is overweight and 30+ is obese.

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height² (m²)

Quick answers

How do I work out a percentage without a calculator?

Divide the percentage by 100 to get a decimal, then multiply by the number. 15% of 240 is 0.15 × 240 = 36. For a quick estimate, 10% is just the number moved one decimal place, and 5% is half of that.

Can I use the calculator with a keyboard only?

Yes. Numbers, + − × ÷, Enter and Esc all work, and the expanded scientific keypad accepts typed expressions such as sin(30)+sqrt(16).

Does the AI show its working?

It is asked to. Ask a question in the chat and it returns the steps, not just the answer — handy for homework checks where the method matters as much as the result.

Questions people ask

Is Inquiry Calculator free to use?

Yes. Every calculator and the AI solver are free, with no account, sign-up or download required.

Can it solve a maths problem from a photo or a screenshot?

Yes. Use the Snapshot button on the AI page to capture a browser tab, or attach a photo of a worksheet, and the AI will read the problem and explain the working step by step.

Does the scientific calculator work in degrees or radians?

Both. The Deg/Rad key switches the trigonometry mode, and results update as you type.

Can it solve an equation for x?

Yes — switch the calculator to Solve. Type the equation with the x key and press SOLVE: 14400=x×0.08×3, 2=x+2, 3x+4=2x−1, x²−5x+6=0, sin(x)=0.5, e^x=5, 5000×(1+x)^8=8000. It searches numerically, so it finds real roots rather than rearranging the algebra, works at any scale from 0.0001 to millions, and offers an exact fraction when one is close (x = 3/2). Every answer is checked by substituting it back into both sides. In Calculate mode nothing changes — the keypad stays an ordinary calculator.

Are the loan and savings calculators accurate?

They use the standard amortisation and compound interest formulas used by lenders, so they are a close estimate. Fees, offset accounts and rate changes are not included.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to phones and tablets, and the keypad is sized for touch.
